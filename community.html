Community

Working with communities.

Community work begins with the everyday issues that affect how people live, work and move around the city. In Bukit Bintang, that means listening to residents, working with local communities and engaging government agencies to improve our neighbourhoods.

01 · NEIGHBOURHOODS

Improving the places we call home.

From city-centre neighbourhoods to residential communities, much of the work begins with practical local issues and finding ways to bring residents and government agencies together.

Infrastructure & Public Amenities

Working on neighbourhood infrastructure, public facilities and everyday improvements that make communities safer, cleaner and more liveable.

Cleanliness & Environment

Supporting cleaner neighbourhoods through community programmes, waste management initiatives and cooperation between residents, businesses and local authorities.

Mobility & Connectivity

Advocating for safer pedestrian connections, better access to public transport and improvements that make it easier for people to move around Kuala Lumpur.

Responsive Local Government

Raising local concerns with the relevant agencies and encouraging practical solutions to issues affecting residents and businesses.

02 · EDUCATION & YOUTH

Creating opportunities for the next generation.

Education, youth development and participation in community life are important foundations for stronger neighbourhoods.

Education

Supporting schools, students and education initiatives that expand opportunities for young people in the community.

Youth Programmes

Creating opportunities for young people to participate in community programmes, leadership activities and local initiatives.

Sports

Supporting sports and recreational programmes that bring communities together and encourage healthy, active lifestyles.

Community Participation

Encouraging young people to take part in the life of their neighbourhoods and contribute ideas to their communities.

03 · COMMUNITY WELFARE

Supporting communities where it matters.

Strong communities depend on people looking out for one another. Community initiatives can help connect residents with assistance, resources and organisations able to provide support.

Food & Community Support

Working with community partners on food distribution and support initiatives for families and residents who need assistance.

Community Partnerships

Bringing together government agencies, civil society, businesses and community organisations to address local needs.

Resident Engagement

Listening to residents and working with local community representatives to identify issues and practical solutions.

Inclusive Communities

Supporting programmes that bring people from different backgrounds and neighbourhoods together.

04 · A BETTER KUALA LUMPUR

Thinking beyond individual neighbourhoods.

Local community work also raises larger questions about how Kuala Lumpur should develop and how the city can become more liveable, inclusive and responsive to the people who call it home.

Urban Governance

Exploring how stronger local institutions and greater community participation can improve decision-making in Kuala Lumpur.

Public Spaces

Supporting streets, public spaces and community facilities that are welcoming, accessible and useful to the people who use them.

Sustainability

Encouraging practical approaches to recycling, waste reduction and environmentally responsible city development.

A Liveable City

Focusing on the everyday experience of Kuala Lumpur — connectivity, cleanliness, safety, accessibility and the quality of neighbourhood life.
